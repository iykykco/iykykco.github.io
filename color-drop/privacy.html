Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Color Drop ("the app") is made by IYKYK CO ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It works fully offline.

What stays on your device

The app saves your level progress, stars, earned boosters and settings (sound, vibration, reduce motion) in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress or by deleting the app.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, usage data, crash data or purchase data. The app does not use the camera, microphone or location.

Contacting us

If you email us, we will receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We will not share them, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you from the app, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete. Residents of places with privacy laws (such as the EU/UK GDPR or US state laws) may still contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, note it in the app update.
